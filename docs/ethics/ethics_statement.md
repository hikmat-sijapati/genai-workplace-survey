# Ethics Statement

**Study title:** Generative AI in the Workplace: Productivity and Job Security Perceptions

## Participant Population
Working professionals aged 18 and over, recruited via professional networks (LinkedIn,
alumni groups, personal contacts). No vulnerable populations (minors, employees of the
researcher, or individuals in a dependent relationship with the researcher) are targeted.

## Consent
Participation is voluntary. An informed consent statement is presented as the first item of
the survey; participants must actively agree before proceeding (see
`docs/questionnaire/Survey_Questionnaire.docx`, Introduction section). Participants may exit
the survey at any point without submitting their responses.

## Anonymity & Confidentiality
- No names, email addresses, or employer names are collected.
- No IP address or device identifiers are stored in the analysis dataset.
- Responses are aggregated and reported only at the group level; no individual response is
  quoted in a way that could identify a respondent.
- Open-text responses (E1, E2) are screened before analysis and any inadvertently identifying
  detail is redacted.

## Data Storage & Security
- Raw export is stored in `data/raw/` on a password-protected device and is not committed to
  the public GitHub repository if it contains any residual identifying detail.
- The processed, fully de-identified dataset (`data/processed/survey_clean.csv`) is the only
  version shared publicly, in line with reproducible-research practice.
- Data will be retained only for the duration of the course assessment and deleted
  afterward, per institutional guidance.

## Risks & Benefits
The study poses minimal risk (comparable to everyday workplace surveys). Questions about
job-security anxiety are non-clinical in nature; participants are free to skip any item.
There is no direct benefit to participants; the study contributes to academic understanding
of workplace AI adoption.

## Right to Withdraw
Because the survey is anonymous, participants cannot be identified after submission and
therefore cannot request withdrawal of a specific response post-submission. This limitation
is disclosed in the consent text.
